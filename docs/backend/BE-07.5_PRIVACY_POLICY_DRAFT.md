# Privacy Policy update for BE-07 — approval draft

**Status: not approved; do not enable public enquiry submission until the owner approves the missing policy decisions and publishes the final text.** The public `/private-policy` page remains unchanged while approval is pending.

## Proposed English copy

### Enquiries and catalogue requests

When you submit a contact enquiry, we collect your name, phone number, email address, any company or project name you provide, your message, and limited source information identifying the page and form placement. When you request a catalogue, we also collect the catalogue identifier and the professional role you select, including an optional role description if you choose “Other”. We use these details to record and respond to your request and, for a catalogue request, to provide the catalogue file.

Enquiries are stored in the website’s application database and may be made available to authorised Pots & Planters personnel who handle enquiries. If Telegram notifications are enabled, relevant enquiry details are sent through the configured Telegram bot to the chat recipients configured by the site administrator. Telegram and its infrastructure process those messages under their own terms. A catalogue download uses a short-lived access token; the token is not part of the download URL and is not retained in browser storage.

**[OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED: confirm which business entity controls this information and whether the listed personnel and Telegram recipients are the complete set of people or service providers with access.]**

### Retention

Enquiry records and their delivery history are retained in the application database. The current application does not automatically delete enquiry records. Catalogue download access tokens expire after 15 minutes. Idempotency records used to safely process a repeated form submission expire after 24 hours.

**[OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED: set and approve the retention period and deletion process for enquiry records, delivery history, and Telegram messages. The current application has no automatic enquiry-retention cleanup.]**

### Privacy questions

For questions about a request or personal information, contact us using the details on the [Contact Us](/contacts) page.

**[OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED: confirm that the Contact Us page identifies the correct privacy contact and is an acceptable channel for access, correction, or deletion requests.]**
