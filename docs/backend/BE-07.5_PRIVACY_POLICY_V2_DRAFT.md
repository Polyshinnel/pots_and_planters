## Proposed English copy for `/private-policy`

### Enquiries and catalogue requests

When you submit a contact enquiry, we collect your name, phone number, email address, any company or project name you provide, your message, and limited information about the page or form from which you contacted us. When you request a catalogue, we also collect the catalogue identifier and the professional role you select, including any additional role description you provide if you select “Other”.

We use this information to record, review and respond to your enquiry and, where applicable, provide access to the requested catalogue. Enquiries are stored in our application database and are accessible to authorised personnel who handle them.

If Telegram notifications are enabled, relevant enquiry information may be sent by our Telegram bot to the recipients designated by authorised site administrators. Those recipients may include individual chats and group chats. Telegram processes messages using its own services and infrastructure, which may involve processing outside your country. We limit access to these chats to people authorised to handle enquiries.

Catalogue downloads are made available through short-lived access tokens, valid for 15 minutes. Download tokens are not included in download URLs and are not stored in browser storage.

### Retention of enquiry information

Our intended retention period for enquiry records is **24 months after the last interaction relating to the enquiry**, unless a longer period is required to comply with applicable law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. We intend to delete or anonymise records after the applicable retention period, subject to those exceptions.

**Implementation note (not for publication):** Automated deletion or anonymisation of enquiry records and delivery history is not yet implemented. The retention statement above must not be published as an operational commitment until a corresponding process is implemented and verified. A separate Telegram-message retention and deletion procedure is also required.

Short-lived catalogue download tokens expire after 15 minutes. Idempotency records used to recognise repeated submissions expire after 24 hours.

### Your privacy questions and requests

For questions about personal information you have submitted, or to request access, correction or deletion where applicable, please contact us using the details on our [Contact Us](/contacts) page. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law.

### Responsible business

The business responsible for handling enquiry information is **DEVA HARMONY TRADING**.
