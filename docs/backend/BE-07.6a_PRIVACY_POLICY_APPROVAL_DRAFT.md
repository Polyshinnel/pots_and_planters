## Proposed English Privacy Policy

# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [date of publication]**

This Privacy Policy explains how Pots &amp; Planters handles information when you visit this website, contact us, submit an enquiry, or request a catalogue.

## Who is responsible for your information

The website is operated by **DEVA HARMONY TRADING [confirm the exact registered legal name and that this entity is the data controller]** (referred to in this Policy as “Pots &amp; Planters”, “we”, “us” or “our”). Our business serves customers in the United Arab Emirates. Contact details for privacy questions are available on our [Contact Us](/contacts) page.

## Information we collect

When online enquiry submission is available and you submit a contact or catalogue request, we may collect:

- your name, telephone number and email address;
- a company or project name, if you provide one;
- the message or project details you enter in a contact form;
- for a catalogue request, the catalogue you selected and the professional role you select, including a description if you choose “Other”;
- the website page and form placement from which you submitted the request; and
- information needed to process the request, maintain its status and record its handling, such as an enquiry reference, status changes and internal notes.

The website currently stores an enquiry cart in your browser’s local storage. It contains product identifiers, SKU values and quantities you select. It does not currently submit product or cart enquiries: these submission types remain unavailable while the product catalogue is being developed. If that changes, this Policy will be reviewed and updated before those submissions are enabled.

Our website and service infrastructure may also process technical information needed to deliver and protect the service, such as request details and network information used for security controls and rate limits. The precise information and retention periods in hosting, proxy, application and backup logs depend on the configured service providers and require operational confirmation.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in an enquiry unless it is necessary for us to respond to your request.

## How we use information

We use enquiry information to:

- receive, record and respond to your contact or catalogue request;
- provide the catalogue file you requested;
- discuss the relevant products, project or business requirements;
- manage the enquiry, including its status and related internal handling notes;
- protect the website and prevent abuse; and
- meet applicable record-keeping or legal requirements, where they apply.

A catalogue request is recorded before a download is made available. The download uses a short-lived access grant that expires after 15 minutes. The grant token is provided to the browser for the download request and is not placed in the download URL or browser local storage.

## Who may receive information

Enquiry records are stored in the website application database and may be accessed by authorised personnel who manage or respond to enquiries. Access to the administration interface is restricted to authorised administrators.

If Telegram notifications are enabled by an administrator, relevant enquiry details are sent through a configured Telegram bot to recipients enabled by an administrator. A notification may include your name, phone number, email address, company, selected role, message and the requested catalogue title, where provided. Only recipients designated and enabled by an administrator are configured to receive these notifications. The recipient list can change over time.

Telegram processes messages through its own service and infrastructure under its terms and privacy information. A message delivered to Telegram may be retained or copied in Telegram or recipient accounts; deleting a record from our application does not delete a Telegram message. Do not include sensitive information in a message unless needed for your request.

We may also rely on providers that operate or support website hosting, storage, databases, queues and security controls. The providers, their processing locations, retention periods and any international transfer arrangements must be confirmed by the business before publication. We do not sell enquiry information.

## International processing

Our website serves customers in the United Arab Emirates. Telegram and other infrastructure providers may process information in countries outside the UAE, depending on their services and configuration. The exact locations and applicable safeguards have not yet been confirmed for this Policy. We will provide accurate information about relevant providers and transfer arrangements once these have been verified.

## How long we keep information

We intend to retain enquiry records for **24 months after the last interaction concerning the enquiry**, then delete or irreversibly anonymise them, subject to applicable legal retention requirements, disputes, or a legal hold. The operational process for applying this period is to be implemented and verified separately; this statement must not be treated as proof that automatic deletion is already active.

The 24-month period applies to the enquiry and its related handling records, including internal notes and notification delivery history, to the extent they contain or identify personal information. Some records may need to be retained longer where required by law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Telegram copies are controlled by Telegram and recipient accounts and are not removed by deleting our application records.

The catalogue download grant expires after 15 minutes. A technical idempotency record used to prevent duplicate form submissions expires after 24 hours. The enquiry cart remains in your browser’s local storage until you remove it, clear browser storage, or your browser removes it. The cookie notice preference is stored in local storage for six calendar months after you accept it.

## Cookies and browser storage

The current frontend uses browser local storage for essential website features: the enquiry cart and the “I agree” cookie notice preference. These entries are stored on your device and are not browser cookies. The cookie notice preference expires six calendar months after it is recorded; the cart has no automatic expiry in the current frontend. You can clear local storage through your browser settings, but doing so removes saved cart contents and the notice preference.

The current frontend code reviewed for this draft does not include analytics or advertising pixels or embedded third-party widgets. Links to services such as WhatsApp or map directions are optional; if you open them, the relevant provider may receive information under its own terms. Your browser and device may separately use cookies or other storage.

## Keeping information secure

We use access controls and technical measures intended to protect information handled by the website. Administrator access is restricted, Telegram bot and proxy secrets are stored using application encryption, and catalogue download grants are time-limited. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. The business should confirm its current access review, backup, logging and incident-response arrangements before publication.

## Your privacy requests

Depending on applicable law and your circumstances, you may be able to ask us about personal information associated with you, request access or correction, ask for deletion, or raise an objection or complaint. We will review each request under the law that applies and any relevant legal retention obligations; this Policy does not promise that every request can be granted.

To make a privacy request, please use the contact details on our [Contact Us](/contacts) page and identify the request as a privacy matter. We may need to verify your identity and ask for information needed to locate the relevant enquiry. The business must confirm that the published contacts are monitored and that requests received there can be routed to a responsible person.

## Third-party websites and services

This website may link to external services, including WhatsApp, map providers and Telegram. We do not control those services. Their own terms and privacy notices apply when you use them. Review those notices before providing information directly to a third party.

## Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in the website, our data handling or applicable requirements. The current version and its effective date will be shown on this page.

## Contact

For questions about this Policy or to make a privacy request, please use the contact details on our [Contact Us](/contacts) page.

## Audit notes and unresolved owner questions

### Findings from the current page and code

- The live source is `frontend/src/app/private-policy/page.tsx`; it uses the existing `/private-policy` route and `getStaticPageMetadata('private_policy')`. It currently says form contents are not sent and that there is no form-submission retention period. Those statements are stale relative to the implemented API and must be replaced before public submissions are enabled.
- `POST /api/v1/enquiries` stores supported contact and catalogue requests in MariaDB. Stored fields include name, phone, email, optional company/message, catalogue role/role description and catalogue reference/snapshot, source page/placement, enquiry status and submission time. Filament permits administrators to add internal notes and status history.
- `product_enquiry` and `cart_enquiry` are rejected by the backend as unavailable and the frontend keeps submission disabled. The browser cart stores product IDs, SKU values and quantities in local storage without an automatic expiry.
- Telegram delivery is conditional on administrator settings. Each new enquiry snapshots enabled recipient IDs, names and chat IDs for delivery. The message can contain the customer contact details and submitted message. Retrying a delivery may create a duplicate in Telegram in the documented crash-after-send case; deleting local records would not delete messages held by Telegram or recipients.
- Catalogue download grants are hash-stored server side, expire after 15 minutes, and are transmitted to the browser in the API response for a bearer-authenticated download. The frontend keeps the token in component memory and does not put it in a URL or local storage.
- Idempotency key and payload hashes are stored for 24 hours. The browser cookie notice preference and enquiry cart are local-storage entries, not browser cookies. The preference expires after six calendar months; the cart does not expire automatically.
- `/contacts` provides a privacy-request route, but actual published contact values come from the contacts API/settings. This code review cannot verify that current contact details are correct, monitored, or operationally routed for privacy requests.
- `BE-07.5_PRIVACY_POLICY_V2_DRAFT.md` is now present and records the same controller placeholder, 24-month retention target, `/contacts` route, Telegram recipients, 15-minute grants, and 24-hour idempotency expiry. It is explicitly a partial insert, not a full replacement. This BE-07.6a document reconciles those points with the complete current page and the code paths. Its retention implementation and Telegram administration gates remain open and are reflected here.
- The V2 copy says Telegram recipients may include individual and group chats; the code stores signed chat IDs and snapshots enabled recipients, so both are supported. Telegram messages include name, phone, email, company, selected role, message and catalogue title when present. The optional `roleOther` description is stored in the application but is not currently included in the Telegram message.
- No production database, deployment, public policy content, environment flag, or Telegram message was changed as part of BE-07.6a.
