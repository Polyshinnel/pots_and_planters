## Proposed public-facing English copy

# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [publication date]**

This Privacy Policy explains how Pots & Planters handles information when you visit this website, contact us, submit an enquiry, or request a catalogue.

## Who is responsible for your information

**DEVA HARMONY TRADING** operates this website and is identified by the business as responsible for the personal information described in this Policy (referred to as “Pots & Planters”, “we”, “us” or “our”). Its registered jurisdiction and legal address are being verified. For privacy questions, please use our [Contact Us](/contacts) page.

## Information we collect

When you submit a contact enquiry or catalogue request, we collect information you provide, which may include:

- your name, phone number and email address;
- a company or project name, where provided;
- your message and project details;
- the requested catalogue and your selected professional role, including an optional description for “Other”;
- the originating website page and form placement; and
- records used to handle the enquiry, including its reference, status, internal notes and handling history.

The website also stores an enquiry cart in your browser’s local storage, including selected product identifiers, SKUs and quantities. Product and cart enquiry submissions are not yet available; we will review this Policy before enabling them.

Our hosting and application systems may process technical request and network information needed to operate and protect the website, including security and rate-limiting information. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in an enquiry unless necessary for your request.

## How we use information

We use this information to receive and respond to enquiries, provide requested catalogues, discuss projects and products, manage enquiry status and internal handling, protect the service from misuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

A catalogue request is recorded before access to the file is provided. The download is protected by a short-lived access token that expires after 15 minutes. The token is not included in the download URL or stored in browser local storage.

## Hosting and recipients

Our website, application servers, database, administration interface and supporting application infrastructure are hosted with **Timeweb on servers located in the Netherlands**. Authorised Pots & Planters personnel may access enquiries to manage and respond to requests. The Filament administration interface is part of our own application, not a separate external service provider.

If enabled, our Telegram bot sends enquiry notifications to recipients designated and enabled by an administrator. These notifications may include your name, phone number, email address, company, selected professional role, message and requested catalogue title, where provided. Internal administrative notes, download tokens and application secrets are not intended to be included in these notifications.

A designated Pots & Planters administrator manages the recipient list and access to company-controlled chats, and handles appropriate message-deletion requests to the extent supported by Telegram. Telegram operates its own infrastructure and may retain messages or copies in recipient accounts. Removing an enquiry from our database does not remove its Telegram messages. We do not sell your personal information.

## International data processing

Our primary website infrastructure is hosted in the **Netherlands (European Union)** by Timeweb. The locations used for backups, provider-level logs and other supporting services require operational verification. Telegram may process or store notifications in countries outside the United Arab Emirates and the European Union. Applicable international-transfer requirements and safeguards will be reviewed before production activation of the relevant processing.

## How long we keep information

Our approved retention policy is **24 months after the last interaction concerning an enquiry** for enquiry records, related internal notes, status and handling history, and notification delivery records, unless longer retention is legally required or needed for a dispute or legal hold. At the end of the applicable period, personal information is to be deleted or irreversibly anonymised. This retention and deletion process is subject to technical implementation and verification before this statement is published as an operational practice.

Our approved maximum retention periods for **backups and technical logs are 30 days**, subject to verification and enforcement in our hosting and application configuration. Data in backups may remain until the applicable backup expires. Telegram messages and copies held by recipients are not automatically deleted by our website; the designated administrator handles removal requests within Telegram's available controls.

Catalogue download grants expire after 15 minutes. Technical idempotency records used to prevent duplicate submissions expire after 24 hours. The enquiry cart remains in your browser’s local storage until you remove it or clear browser storage, or your browser removes it. The cookie notice preference is kept in local storage for six calendar months after acceptance.

## Cookies and browser storage

The frontend uses browser local storage for the enquiry cart and the cookie notice preference. These entries are not browser cookies. The cart has no automatic expiry; the notice preference expires after six calendar months. You can clear these entries through your browser settings.

The frontend reviewed for this draft does not contain analytics or advertising pixels or embedded third-party widgets. Optional links to WhatsApp or map services may lead to third-party sites governed by their own notices. We will review this section if the site's integrations change.

## Security

We use access controls and technical measures intended to protect enquiry information. Administration access is restricted to authorised users, Telegram bot and proxy credentials are encrypted within the application, and catalogue download access is time-limited. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## Your privacy requests

Depending on applicable law and your circumstances, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with you, or raise a privacy concern. We assess requests in light of applicable legal obligations and may need to verify your identity.

Please use the current contact details on our [Contact Us](/contacts) page and identify your enquiry as a privacy request. These contact details have been confirmed by the business as current.

## Third-party websites and services

Our website may link to third-party services, including WhatsApp and map providers. Their own privacy notices apply when you use their services.

## Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as our services, processing or legal obligations change. The current version and its effective date will appear on this page.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the details on our [Contact Us](/contacts) page.
