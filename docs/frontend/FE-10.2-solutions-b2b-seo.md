## Goal
Improve the existing `/solutions` page's search relevance and usefulness for architects, interior designers, hospitality operators, contractors and procurement teams **without redesigning the approved Interactive Solutions Gallery**. Preserve the editorial layout, demo scenes, hotspots, product specifications, cart actions, and mobile behavior.

## Before editing
1. Inspect current `/solutions` route, metadata, server/client component boundaries, typed solution data, images, links, product associations, and shared SEO utilities.
2. Identify where copy belongs (data module vs. presentational components). Keep editorial copy and metadata centrally managed, ideally compatible with a future Laravel/Filament-backed content model.
3. Do not overwrite manual design changes. Do not assume current file paths: inspect the repository.

## Page hero
- Keep the existing H1 **Planters & Greenery for Every Space** and editorial image/layout.
- Keep the introductory message concise and project-oriented. Suggested text:

  > Explore planter and artificial greenery solutions for hotels, restaurants, offices, villas and commercial spaces. Discover interior concepts, review suitable products and build an enquiry for your project.

## Five solution sections: semantic headings and approved copy
Each section needs one descriptive **H2** (SEO intent), while the existing expressive heading can be retained as a smaller visual subheading or eyebrow without creating competing H2s. Avoid making the section visually taller than necessary. Preserve the existing anchors, category links and scene galleries.
